Read the event rules

Challenge values appear on each challenge. Static challenges keep their value. Dynamic challenges can decrease in value as more participants solve them.

For dynamic challenges, your total reflects the current value of previous solves. Unlocking a paid hint deducts its displayed cost. Check the cost before confirming.

  • Stay in scope. Attack only the targets explicitly provided by a challenge. Do not attack the event infrastructure.
  • Keep flags within your team. Do not share flags or solutions with other participants or teams.
  • Respect submission limits. Do not automate spam submissions. Challenge limits and rate limits apply.
  • Follow organiser decisions. Rule violations can lead to penalties or disqualification. The organisers' decisions are final.

Contact the organisers through the event's official communication channel. Include the challenge name, what happened, and the time of the issue so they can investigate.
